[[Smelter]] output: the standard stock every Fabricator blueprint builds on.

| Material | Id | From | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Iron Ingot | `iron_ingot` | Iron Ore | Universal structural input: frames, segments, valves, half the Fabricator catalogue |
| Glass | `glass` | Silicon | Sheet stock for panels, housings, optics |
| Titanium Ingot | `titanium_ingot` | Titanium | High-strength, lightweight stock for rotors, thrusters, pressure hardware |
| Cobalt Ingot | `cobalt_ingot` | Cobalt | Battery-grade stock; every Battery Cell is built around it |
| Rare Earth Core | `rare_earth_core` | Rare Earth | Precision magnetic core for control units and advanced electronics |
| Neutronium Bar | `neutronium_bar` | Neutronium | Ultra-dense bar stock for Neutron Capacitors |
| Lead Ingot | `lead_ingot` | Lead Ore | Shielding stock; rolls into Lead Plate for casks and reactor hardware |

Full input/output relationships live in [[Fabricator Recipes]].
