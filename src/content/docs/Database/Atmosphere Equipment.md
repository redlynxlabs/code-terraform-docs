Atmosphere generators and their upgrade packs. Product names match the Shop and Inventory.

## Generators

| Item | Id | Role | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oxygen Generator | `oxygen_generator` | Converts atmospheric CO₂ into breathable oxygen. See [[Oxygen Generator]] | 1,000 cr |
| Heat Generator | `temp_heater` | Radiates heat to warm the surface. See [[Heat Generator]] | 800 cr |
| Pressure Generator | `pressure_generator` | Compresses gas to raise surface pressure. See [[Pressure Generator]] | 900 cr |
| Weather Station | `weather_station` | Programmable 50 W local weather station with live reception and Signal Board. See [[Weather Station]] | 60,000 cr |

## Upgrade packs

Each pack upgrades **one deployed generator**; every machine needs its own pack.

| Pack | Effect | Fluid/fuel | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oxygen Mk II (`oxygen_upgrade_pack_mk2`) | 5× output, 5× power | none | 12,000 cr |
| Heat Mk II (`heat_upgrade_pack_mk2`) | 4.7× output, 5× power | none | 12,000 cr |
| Pressure Mk II (`pressure_upgrade_pack_mk2`) | 5× output, 5× power | none | 12,000 cr |
| Oxygen Mk III (`oxygen_upgrade_pack_mk3`) | 200× Mk I output, 20× Mk I power | 8 t/h Water (falls back to Mk II output without it) | 90,000 cr |
| Heat Mk III (`heat_upgrade_pack_mk3`) | 208× Mk I output, 20× Mk I power | 12 t/h Steam (falls back to Mk II) | 90,000 cr |
| Pressure Mk III (`pressure_upgrade_pack_mk3`) | 200× Mk I output, 20× Mk I power | 5 t/h Water (falls back to Mk II) | 90,000 cr |
| Oxygen Mk IV (`oxygen_upgrade_pack_mk4`) | 500× Mk I output, 100× Mk I power | 4-Fuel-Rod magazine from a connected [[Lead Cask]]; ~240 h per rod, stops when spent and empty | 180,000 cr (also fabricable) |
| Heat Mk IV (`heat_upgrade_pack_mk4`) | 515× Mk I output, 100× Mk I power | same 4-rod magazine | 180,000 cr (fabricable) |
| Pressure Mk IV (`pressure_upgrade_pack_mk4`) | 500× Mk I output, 100× Mk I power | same 4-rod magazine | 180,000 cr (fabricable) |
