---

The [[Fuel Assembler]] makes Fuel Rods and Nuclear Batteries from Raw Uranium and lead plates. **Uranium enters from a [[Lead Cask]]; Fuel Rods leave through the hot lane**, while Nuclear Batteries use ordinary product storage. Both recipes are Production tier T2.

| Recipe id | Inputs → Output | Time | Power | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `craft_fuel_rod` | 4× Raw Uranium, 2× Lead Plate → 1× Fuel Rod | 6 h | 1,800 W | Order: Vestibule, Hot Freight Proof |
| `craft_nuclear_battery` | 4× Raw Uranium, 3× Lead Plate → 1× Nuclear Battery | 4 h | 1,200 W | Order: Helios, Reactor Vessel Chain |
