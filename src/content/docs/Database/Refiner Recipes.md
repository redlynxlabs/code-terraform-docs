The [[Refiner]] purifies raw exotic fluids into creature-grade form, burning **`tar` as the refining reagent**: raw fluid in, refined fluid out. Blueprints unlock through Bio Orders. All are Production tier T2.

| Recipe id | Inputs → Output | Time | Power | Unlocking Bio Order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `refine_sulfur_gas` | 2× Tar, 4 t Raw Sulfur Gas → 4 t Sulfur Gas | 0.3 h | 30 W | Eruption-Zone Capstone |
| `refine_cryofluid` | 2× Tar, 4 t Raw Cryofluid → 4 t Cryofluid | 0.3 h | 30 W | Cryofluid Refinement Protocol |
| `refine_chlorine` | 5× Tar, 4 t Raw Chlorine → 4 t Chlorine | 0.4 h | 48 W | Geothermal Grand Compendium |
| `refine_quicksilver` | 5× Tar, 4 t Raw Quicksilver → 4 t Quicksilver | 0.4 h | 48 W | Abyssal Grand Compendium |
