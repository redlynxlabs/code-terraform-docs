# Biosphere Equipment

Plant, habitat, husbandry, and biome-processing hardware, including deploy kits and upgrade packs.

## Machines and kits

| Item | Id | Role | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plant Terraformer Kit | `plant_terraformer_kit` | Deploys a [[Plant Terraformer]]; every machine adds its tier conversion rate × outpost efficiency to the shared Plants pillar | Fabricated |
| Crop Automator Kit | `crop_automator_kit` | Deploys a [[Crop Automator]] (24-cell service area) | 30,000 cr |
| Portable Bio Scanner | `portable_bio_scanner` | Drone module: scans the current surface tile for life forms, records to the [[Journal]] | 10,000 cr |
| Portable Bio Extractor | `portable_bio_extractor` | Drone module: harvests scanned life forms; 25 t sealed one-type chamber, Cargo Pods add capacity | 14,000 cr |
| Essence Liquifier | `essence_liquifier` | Life-form samples → biome essence. See [[Essence Liquifier]] | 35,000 cr |
| Biomass Mixer | `biomass_mixer` | Essences → biomass. See [[Biomass Mixer]] | 60,000 cr |
| Habitat | `habitat` | Revives and houses one creature species at a time; established colonies can move without losing progress. See [[Habitat]] | 100,000 cr |
| Feed Maker | `feed_maker` | Crafts creature feeds. See [[Feed Maker]] | 50,000 cr |
| Refiner | `refiner` | Raw exotic feedstock + tar → creature-grade fluid. See [[Refiner]] | 80,000 cr |
