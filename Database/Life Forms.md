# Life Forms

Native life forms gathered across the five biomes. They liquify into biome essences at the [[Essence Liquifier]] (common ×5, uncommon ×10, rare ×25 essence yield) and feature in creature feed recipes. Collect them with a [[Drone]]'s bio scanner and bio extractor.
